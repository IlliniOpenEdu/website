About Our Mission

IlliniOpenEdu exists to make STEM education more accessible through practical, open-source learning tools. Our student-led teams create resources that support both classroom instruction and independent self-study.

By combining pedagogy, design, and engineering, we help educators and learners access high-quality educational technology without paywalls.
